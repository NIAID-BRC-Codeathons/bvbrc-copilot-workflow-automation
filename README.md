## Goal (proposed)

Advance and systematically evaluate BV-BRC Copilot for generating, executing, and reproducing bioinformatics workflows from research questions and published methods.

## Three-Day MVP (proposed)

Build a benchmark of bioinformatics tasks spanning assembly, annotation, comparative genomics, phylogeny, AMR, RNA-seq, and data discovery. Use BV-BRC Copilot and MCP-enabled agents to generate and execute workflows while capturing parameters, data, provenance, logs, and replay instructions.

As a reproducibility challenge, select published studies and have Copilot reconstruct their analyses from methods sections, identifying data, tools, parameters, and workflow steps and reporting unresolved information as a reproducibility gap report.

## Evaluation (proposed)

Workflow completion and scientific correctness; reproducibility on rerun; provenance completeness; recovery of methods, tools, and parameters from publications; agreement with published results; hallucination/error rate; and human intervention required.
